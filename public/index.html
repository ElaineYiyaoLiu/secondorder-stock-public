Method and limitations

Test origins start after 260 observations. Candidate endpoints are sampled every five observations. Every candidate window and its forward outcome finish before the query window begins. Neighbours are separated by the window plus the outcome horizon. MAE is measured in return percentage points; lower is better. The baseline is the mean of spaced, mature past returns. All enabled methods are compared at shared scored origins; available counts show coverage before pairing.

This is a small exploratory comparison, without statistical significance tests, trading costs, or a point-in-time constituent universe. Imported data must be adjusted consistently for splits and distributions. Existing tickers and short histories cannot support claims of investable alpha.
