New to stocks? Read the numbers

Return = price change in percent. Volume = shares traded. Volatility = how much daily returns vary, not expected profit. Drawdown = a fall from a previous peak. Correlation ranges from −1 (opposite moves) through 0 (little linear relationship) to +1 (same direction). Median = the middle observation. N/A means the data do not support this value.

Method

Robust linear and rank correlation distances feed exact Rips H₀/H₁ diagrams. Diagrams lose asset identity. Each outcome horizon has its own mature cohort. References are separated by the window plus horizon. Sensitivity uses eight joint block resamples against a fixed shortlist of at most 30 candidates per horizon.

These views share data. Multiscale state summarizes the same observations; it is not an independent vote.

Interpretation

Candidate outcomes finish before the query begins. Queries with insufficient references or unstable local matching abstain. The baseline uses mature spaced past returns. MAE is average absolute return error; lower is better. pp means percentage points, so 5% versus 3% differs by 2 pp. Small samples and lower MAE do not establish trading value. The default fixture is synthetic.
